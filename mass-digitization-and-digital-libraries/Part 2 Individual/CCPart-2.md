# Part 2: Reviews and Shifts in Gaming - Charles Cantrell

## Oldest Digital Library/Archive: 
- https://www.mobygames.com/
- moby games collects/hosts reviews, disscussion, media, and statistics of a variety of gaming related topics like specific games, companies, critics, etc..
- The website has changed hands multiple times since its creation 28 years ago, but it still collects and hosts all these bits of info that I previously mentioned. For reviews as an example the specific reviewers may have changed but its much of the same.
- The use case for this library, specifically for this topic in gaming, can be used to see reviews of similer objects(games/genres) from the past and then those reviews can be compared over time or to modern reviews.
