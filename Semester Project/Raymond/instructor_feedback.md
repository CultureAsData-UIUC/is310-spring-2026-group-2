# Instructor Feedback for Initial Dataset

Hi Raymond,

Your project is looking great so far and I really appreciate the level of detail and organization you have included in this assignment. Your dataset looks good and I particularly like that you have included a rationale for every row.

You are, for the most part, very clear about how you are defining 'mainstream' games here and demonstrate an awareness of the biases that your categories present. You have excellent documentation here.

I have a few suggestions/clarifications:

1. When you are deciding how to choose a primary and sub-genre, how are you making this decision? How will these decisions be made when you begin collecting this data computationally? Choices for these categories will directly affect any genre analysis you do, so be sure to be clear about your rationale for these choices.
2. It is not clear what you mean by 'player attention, public discourse, and platform reach'. How are these measured? Do these end up mattering much when all the games you include need to be popular and best-selling enough to make it onto the reference lists? This needs more explanation.
3. You mention it briefly in your limitations but I think you should be more intentional about noting that 'mainstream' here refers to Western culture and popularity. How far does the 'historical relevance' of these games extend and to what communities?
4. In your next steps doc, you mention that you are considering using an LLM for cleaning and making decisions about genre. This will still require you to quality check your data as a LLM can be inaccurate with its genre labels (or anything else it outputs).

Overall, I think this is looking great. Good job.
