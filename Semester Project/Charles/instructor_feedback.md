# Instructor Feedback for Initial Dataset

Hi Charles,

It is clear that you put a lot of time and energy into collecting your data and I like the focus on the historic shifts in the RPG genre specifically.

A few suggestions or ideas for moving forward:

1. A link to the pages for the games you collected should be included for documentation, transparency, and reproducibility.
2. Is there a particular reason you chose 1994-2023? Is this due to the constraints of IGDB or a choice based on your understanding of the genre? This should be more clear.
3. It looks like you are not collecting the keywords tags from the IGDB pages or any measures of game popularity (# of user ratings,  # of users who have added it to the 'want' 'playing' or 'played' categories, etc.). Collecting the keyword tags could help you understand granular details about the game themes. Popularity measures can contextualize what games the community is most interested in.
4. Is the shift to action oriented gaming the same as the games being "dumbed down" for your analysis? How does the data you collect help you understand this trend?
5. You are noticing that newer games have more metadata attached to them, which presents an issue for understanding more retro games. Something to keep in mind about IGDB is that users create the pages for many of the games. The quality is checked by IGDB before posting but the amount of information is going to vary a lot due to this. What you are measuring here is a bias toward newer or more popular games. You could supplement this if you wanted with information from other sources, or you could look into these biases as a possible research inquiry. IGDB is more a measure of the communities thoughts and opinions than some objective truth claim about the games.
6. I would encourage you to dig deeper into the thematic elements of your data rather than the technical. Open world games are more prevalent in modern games because the technology allows them to be. This does not really tell us much about why certain RPG games are popular.

Overall I think this is on the right track and it seems like you have a pretty solid idea about how to move forward for scaling it up. Good job so far. Let me know if you have any questions.
