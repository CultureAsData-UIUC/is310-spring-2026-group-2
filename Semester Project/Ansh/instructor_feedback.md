You mention that you want to study how games are perceived through genre and tag analysis. How do the Steam genre tags inform us about what the community thinks of games with AI content? I think there are some interesting questions to explore here (for example, do AI generated games tend to have tags that are not as common in games without the AI label? Are there any games that have been revealed to have used AI that do not include these labels on Steam? Do the reviews for these games tend to mention AI? What are they saying about it? However, your driving research interests and questions need to be refined before you can move on to asking meaningful cultural questions about Steam games.
